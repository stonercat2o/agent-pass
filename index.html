How it works

1. Hold a Binance Agent ID — the name.ai identity claimed in the Binance app (Wallet → Agent ID). One per person, non-transferable.

2. Connect the wallet that holds it. This page checks the official Agent ID registry on-chain — no sign-up, no form.

3. Claim once per day. Each claim mints a badge that stays in your wallet forever, and claiming on consecutive days grows your streak.

Claims are free: the contract takes no payment and asks for no token approvals — you only pay the network gas fee for your own transaction. Badges are soulbound: they cannot be transferred or sold, and they carry no monetary value. Contract: view
